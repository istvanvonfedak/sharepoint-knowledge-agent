# Travel Rates 2026

## City allowances
These replace the standard meal allowance in the cities listed. Everywhere else the standard rate in the Expense Reimbursement policy applies.

| City | Hotel cap (EUR per night) | Meals (EUR per day) |
|---|---|---|
| Madrid | 150 | 45 |
| Lisbon | 130 | 40 |
| London | 220 | 60 |
| New York | 300 | 75 |

## Mileage and taxis
Business use of a private car is reimbursed at 0.26 EUR per kilometre. Taxis are reimbursed only when no public transport runs.
