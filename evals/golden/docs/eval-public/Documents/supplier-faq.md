# Supplier FAQ

## Invoices
Send invoices to the accounts payable mailbox with the purchase order number in the subject line. Invoices without a purchase order number are returned.

## Payment terms
Suppliers are paid 45 days after the invoice date, on the next Friday payment run.

Raise a dispute within 10 working days of receiving a payment remittance.
