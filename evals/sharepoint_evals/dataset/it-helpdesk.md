# IT Helpdesk Troubleshooting

## VPN problems
VPN error 809 means the connection is blocked before it is established. Check that UDP ports 500 and 4500 are open on your home router, then reconnect. If it persists, reinstall the VPN profile from the self-service portal.

## Password resets
Reset your password at the self-service portal. Passwords expire every 180 days and cannot repeat your last 12.

## Printers
Office printers appear automatically when you are on the corporate network. Badge release is required for confidential print jobs.
