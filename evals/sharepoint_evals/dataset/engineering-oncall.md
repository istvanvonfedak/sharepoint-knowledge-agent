# Engineering On-Call Handbook

## Rotation
On-call rotates weekly every Monday at 10:00. The primary engineer carries the pager; a secondary engineer is the backup.

## Escalation
If the primary does not acknowledge an alert within 15 minutes, the page escalates automatically to the secondary, and after another 15 minutes to the engineering manager.

## Compensation
On-call engineers receive a flat weekly stipend plus time off in lieu for pages between 22:00 and 06:00.
