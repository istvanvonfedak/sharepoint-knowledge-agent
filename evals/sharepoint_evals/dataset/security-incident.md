# Security Incident Response

## Reporting
If you receive a suspicious or phishing email, do not click any links. Forward it to security@example.com within 1 hour and delete it from your inbox.

## Severity levels
- P1: active breach or data loss, response within 15 minutes, security lead paged.
- P2: confirmed malware on a single device, response within 2 hours.
- P3: suspicious activity without confirmed impact, response next business day.
- P4: policy questions and hygiene reminders.

## After an incident
The security team runs a blameless post-mortem within 5 working days and shares the findings company-wide.
