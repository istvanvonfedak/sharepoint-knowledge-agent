# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 22 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year; anything beyond that expires on 31 March.

## Requesting time off
Submit requests through the HR portal at least 2 weeks before your planned absence. Your manager approves or declines within 3 working days. Requests for more than 10 consecutive days need director approval.

## Sick leave
Notify your manager before 9:00 on the first day of sickness. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive days.
