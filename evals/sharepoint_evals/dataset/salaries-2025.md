# Compensation Bands 2025 (Confidential — HR only)

## Engineering
Level L4 base salary band: 62,000 to 78,000 EUR. Level L5 base salary band: 80,000 to 98,000 EUR. Level L6 base salary band: 100,000 to 125,000 EUR.

## Bonus
Annual bonus target is 10 percent of base for L4 and L5, and 15 percent for L6.
