# Expense Reimbursement

## Submitting claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase. Attach itemised receipts; card slips alone are not accepted.

## Travel and meals
Meals while travelling for work are reimbursed up to 45 EUR per day. Use cost code EXP-2041 for all business travel. Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Payment
Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run, normally within 10 working days.
