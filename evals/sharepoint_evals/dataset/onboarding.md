# New Hire Onboarding

## Before day one
IT ships your laptop to your home address one week before your start date. You will receive an invitation to set up your password manager.

## First day
Your onboarding buddy meets you at 10:00, walks you through the office, and introduces the team. Your buddy is your first point of contact for any question during the first month.

## First week
Complete the mandatory security awareness course and sign the data protection acknowledgement. Book a 1:1 with your manager to agree on 30-60-90 day goals.
